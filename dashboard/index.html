Token Launch Radar

Every protocol that records no token yet, ranked by what its launch would be worth — and what to do about it before it happens.

The model implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity The category multiple is the median market-cap/TVL that the market pays for protocols in the same line of business whose token already trades, taken from this snapshot. Momentum adjusts for 7-day TVL growth, maturity for the years a protocol has run with no token. Open any row to see its full multiplication. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade (WBTC and its kin) are screened out: they are not launches waiting to happen.
